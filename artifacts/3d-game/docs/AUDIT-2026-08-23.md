# RETRACTIONS — do not re-derive these

Loudly, because each one was argued confidently by a lens and each one is wrong. Anything in this list that reappears in a future audit should be thrown out on sight.

1. **`ios/App/App/Info.plist` does NOT say `VOIDLING`.** It says `World Ender`, matching `capacitor.config.ts:9`. Three separate lenses filed this as a **blocker** and one of them reasoned to it through a Capacitor CLI source dive. `qa/iosname.mjs` exists, checks exactly this, additionally bans `/voidling/i` and enforces the 12-char truncation limit, and is a **registered gate step** at `qa/gate.mjs:91` in both `push` and `live`. The three remaining `VOIDLING` strings under `ios/` are a plist comment, a Swift header comment, and an `NSLog`.

2. **The privacy manifest exists and the App Privacy answers are written.** `ios/App/App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` is on disk; `APPSTORE.md:349` carries "App Privacy — the answers, question by question" as a table. Filed as a blocker; closed by `4e59f9d`.

3. **Maple Falls' grass is not a flat `fillRect`.** `island.ts:1789` runs a Maple-only tonal pass (52 matched patches per grassy block at 0.15 alpha, ~145× the alpha-times-area of the base mottle) and `:2592` runs a leaf-drift pass Pirate Bay does not have, both *after* the biome fills. The lens that filed this measured `qa/out/shippedlook/maple_firstlook.png` — which is the **before** screenshot named in `ee272a6`'s own commit message. **Do not stack a second lawn pass**: `island.ts` warns twice that this level's exposure has been blown out before by stacking alpha.

4. **Do not retune `GRAIN.maple`.** `island.ts:2782-2790` records it as a *measured negative result*: detail energy is flat-to-DOWN across the whole range inside ±6% noise. `:2692` states Maple, Pirate Bay and Game Day keep the mix they shipped with, by decision.
