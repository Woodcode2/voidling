## 1. The void turns whitish when he is about to be eaten

> "When the void is about to get eaten he turns like whitish for color. Why?"

**ANSWERED, and it is the EYES, not the body.** No body uniform is driven by
mood at all — the purple never changes. What changes is `MOODS.scared`
(`void3d.ts`): `wide: 1.16` enlarges the sclera and `pupil: 0.55` shrinks the
pupil to just over half, so the eye flips from mostly-DARK to mostly-WHITE. At
play size, two big white discs on a small purple ball read as the whole
character going pale. A blue sweat drop and `blush: 0.3` (less pink) finish it.

Rendered side by side: `qa/out/mood/now/mood-cruise.png` against
`mood-scared.png`. Cruise is dark-eyed and warm; scared is two white saucers.

`scared` fires when any rival more than 1.15x your radius is within
`R + rv.r + 16` (`prototype3d.ts`), i.e. it is the game's "something can eat
you" tell.

**So it is authored, and it is not landing.** The owner did not read white
saucer eyes as fear — he read a colour change and asked why. The classic
cartoon version of this keeps the pupil BLACK and merely small; here it also
goes grey, which drops the contrast that would have said "eyes". That is the
thing to fix, not the whiteness itself.

**Not yet fixed** — it is an art call on the character's fear face and belongs
with the owner. Two options to put to him: keep the saucer eyes but hold the
pupil dark, or drop the fear tell to the brow, the sweat and the mouth and
leave the eyes alone.

## 2. Who can eat him, and what does it cost?

> "Also can other voids eat him or just that one? Is there a penalty for being
> eaten?"

**ANSWERED. Any rival bigger than you can bite you; only the HUNTER can hurt
you properly.** From `rivals.ts`, the bite fires on
`rv.r > pr * 1.2 && dp < rv.r * 1.05 && rv.biteCd <= 0` — so it is not one
specific void, it is anyone more than 1.2x your radius. Nothing smaller can
touch you.

There are two grades, and the difference is large:

| | ordinary rival | the HUNTER, mid-hunt |
|---|---|---|
| radius | x0.90 | x0.85, floored one full rung down the form ladder |
| score | nothing | up to 8% stolen, capped at 1200 |
| form | unchanged | **demoted** — the HUD name changes |
| cooldown | 9s | 12s, then she breaks off and wallows |

The stolen score is BANKED by her, so it is winnable back by eating her later —
which is the only event in the game that lifts the growth law.

You also cannot be chain-bitten: `biteMercy` gives 2.5s after an ordinary bite
and 3.2s after the hunter's.

**Nothing to fix here** — but note the demotion was silently rewarding the
player until 2026-08-24, when the EVOLVED ceremony was found firing on the way
back up. That is fixed.

## What "get a team on quality" means here

The studio's eight teams review SURFACES. Nothing in it owns the things above,
which are mostly BEHAVIOUR — a collision response, an effect that fires too
often, a word in a string. A ninth standing team is warranted: one that plays
the game as a child does and reports what is annoying, wrong or confusing,
rather than what is badly drawn.
