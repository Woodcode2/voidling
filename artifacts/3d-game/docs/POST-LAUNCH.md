## The standing hypothesis, filed by the governor 2026-09-02

`src/prototype3d.ts:5365` — `const POWERS_ON = false;   // carved out for
launch — pure drag+eat (hole.io purity)`. There is a powers system, built and
switched off in the name of purity, and the owner's word for the result is
"vanilla". The hypothesis: **the player has no decisions.** For 180 seconds
they hold and drag; every other verb belongs to the rivals, the town, the
clock. "Feeling powerful" is not a bigger number; it is the moment a player
does something *they* chose and the world answers.

Competing readings, none yet tested: no escalation (every match is the same 180
seconds; nothing gets harder because you are good at it); no stakes between
matches; no mastery ceiling; or the loop is fine and polish was the whole gap.

The experiment: turn `POWERS_ON` on in a branch, play five matches, photograph
the moments a child would talk about afterwards. Do not ship it this week.
