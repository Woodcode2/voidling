## THE EIGHT, as work

**1. LANTERN'S DRUM IS BACK — A REGRESSION.** A drum overlapping the music was
fixed once and has returned. Treat as a regression with a probe that FAILS on
today's build: find the original fix in the history, find what undid it, and
land a test that would have caught the return. A fix that can silently revert
was never finished.

**2. THE HUD IS CLUTTERED.** News, chat bubbles, the void window and the
scoreboard compete. His two specific proposals, to be taken seriously rather
than softened: *remove the void window entirely* and let the family speak only
in chat bubbles; and *remove the top-left scoreboard*, because size already
tells you who is winning, with scores reflected at the end instead. GOVERNOR:
these are subtractions, and subtraction is the hardest thing to get an
enthusiastic team to propose. Do not let a crew answer a "remove this" with a
"restyle this".

**3. THE NEWS IS SLOPPY AND NOT FUN — the largest item.** His words: "so
brutal", "two sentences that make no sense", "it feels sloppy", "it's not fun".
His structural note is a real design brief: it should NOT always open solid and
then escalate to panic — that arc is predictable and therefore dead. And: "some
items triggers those news events", i.e. the newsroom should react to what the
player actually ate, not run a timeline. He asked for "a serious writer that
would [work] on triple A games". Take that literally: the deliverable is
WRITING, judged as writing, not a template with more slots.

**4. "TAP TO START" STILL APPEARS.** Game loads, then a tap gate. He believes
it existed to start the music (a browser autoplay requirement). His ask: when
you pick a level and press play, it should start once loaded. GOVERNOR: the
audio-unlock constraint is real in browsers, so the question is whether the tap
already happened earlier in the flow (picking the world IS a tap) and the gate
is now redundant. Prove it from the audio code, do not assume it.

**5. THE VOID NAMES ARE LAME AND CHILDISH.** "Chompzilla etc." Same crew as
the news, same bar: this is a naming problem, and the current names read as
placeholder-grade.

**6. MAPLE MUST ALWAYS BE THE INTRO LEVEL.** The figure-8 finger tutorial shows
on a fresh profile and disappears once there is history — he wants it always on
Maple. And a second, separate bug: "I also see the old version pop up at the
next level where it was like a popup to tell you to move. That must be old
code." Find that popup and confirm whether it is dead code that still fires.

**7. THE SPLASH'S "THE CUTE" LINE IS FADED.** See the screenshot: the white
"THE CUTE" above "WORLD ENDER" is low-contrast against the purple void behind
it. GOVERNOR, unasked: the title also appears TWICE in that frame — once as the
logo lockup and again in yellow beneath it. Worth asking whether the second one
should exist at all.

**8. (GOVERNOR, from the same screenshot.)** The loading line reads "Waking the
void family…" at 0%. Fine, but the whole screen is the first thing a player
ever sees and it has never been reviewed as a composition.
