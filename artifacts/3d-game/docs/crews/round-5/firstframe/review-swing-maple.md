# MAPLE FALLS — the establishing shot, u100 → settled

Cinematographer, Stream D (brief §2D). Pack: `docs/crews/round-5/shots/firstframe/maple_*.png`, numbers from `docs/crews/round-5/firstframe-data/maple.json` (SEED=7, 430x932@2, introLen 2.2).

## VERDICT: NO-SHIP
The shot itself is a real aerial of a real town and the dive lands on the void as designed, but the title card is centred on the exact pixels the void and the DRAG hand arrive on, the full HUD (clock, coin chip, home, growth bar) is live over the whole cinematic, a crowd bubble lands under the card before the controls exist, and on this build the void eats a newspaper box before the child has touched the screen — all four are visible in the page frames and all four close with small, gated changes.

## THE BAR
**Donut County** (Annapurna, iOS 2018, Apple Design Award 2019) — the shipped hole game for a family audience. Every level opens pulled back on the whole scene, holds it for a beat, then eases the camera down onto the hole; the level is captioned small and off-centre, never over the hole; there is no HUD to fight the picture. **Mario Kart Tour** (Nintendo, iOS) for HUD timing: the course flyover plays under the course name with no HUD at all; the HUD arrives with the countdown, when the player's hands matter. **hole.io** (Voodoo) has no establishing shot — it opens on the gameplay camera — so we beat the genre by having one at all.

Where MAPLE FALLS sits: the MOVE is at the bar (pull back on the town, ease onto the void, 2.2 s, no cut). The CARD is below it (centred, over the subject's landing point, opaque past the moment the hand lands). The FRAME is below it (a live HUD over a cinematic, the clock already running). One eat and one bubble fire before the child exists.

## IS THIS THE BEST THIS CAN BE?
No. Between here and the bar, ranked:
1. The card off the subject (upper third) — the single biggest gain; the void and the hand arrive into a clean centre.
2. The HUD dark until the hands matter — the u100 canvas already IS the ad frame; the page frame hides it under a clock and a progress bar.
3. No eat, no gossip before the child exists — the first NOM answers the first drag, the first bubble comes after the name leaves.
4. Then the experiment: hero on the bandstand vs null. The dive itself — 300 → 38, quadratic ease-in, no cut, 2.2 s — is right for Maple: it is the teaching world and the fastest path to the hands, and Donut County's zoom is no slower. The move reads as a reveal, not a fall, because the ground never leaves the frame and the void grows into its face across u75 → u25.
