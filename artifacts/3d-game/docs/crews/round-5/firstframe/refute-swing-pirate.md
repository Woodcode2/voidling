- **The proof was already in the repo.** The paired SEED=7 audit (`s7v-before/after-pirate.json`, commit `7a9ee4c`) shows the hotel disappearing across the settle patch and names the cabana that does it with a measured depth (2.14 > 1.0). - **The match clock runs under the cutscene.** `t = matchElapsed() = matchLen - matchClock` (:1949, :3351) and the timer reads 3:00 / 2:59 / 2:58 / 2:57 across the swing. A child loses 2.2 s of a 180 s match — 3.4 s on Game Day, 3.6 on Lantern — to a shot they cannot move in, and Mario Kart Tour, the review's own bar, starts its countdown after the flyover. Small, but it is the one HUD element the review chose to keep, and it is counting down.
- **The pan crosses the bay.** `pirate_u75_canvas.png` is turquoise water and three boats because the look point at that moment, (84, 70), is inside `BAY_WATER` (bay.ts:24-28) — the hero-to-spawn line is a chord across the bay, so the middle half of the swing has no authored ground under it by geometry, whatever the introLen. - **A prop is inside the eat radius at the authored spawn** (finding 5 above): the child's first eat in Pirate Bay happens with no input, during the card.
