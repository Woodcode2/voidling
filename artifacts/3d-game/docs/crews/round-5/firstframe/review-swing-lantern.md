# LANTERN NIGHT — the establishing shot, u100 → settled

Cinematographer, Stream D (brief §2D). Pack: `docs/crews/round-5/shots/firstframe/lantern_*.png`, numbers from `docs/crews/round-5/firstframe-data/lantern.json` (SEED=7, 430x932@2, introLen 3.6). The code says the subject is the bathhouse — `hero: [(6280 - 6000) * 0.05, (2500 - 6000) * 0.05]` (prototype3d.ts:1430) → 3D (14, −175) — and the void spawns at `LN_SPAWN: Pt = [6620, 10150]` (lantern.ts:218) → 3D (31, 207.5): the subject is 383 units north of the void, the longest hand-over of the five worlds (Game Day's is 280.5).

Every camDist, NDC position and threshold below is arithmetic from the code's own constants (`camDist = 38 + 262 * k2 * k2`, 9493; `q = (u - 0.25) / 0.5; e = q * q * (3 - 2 * q)`, 9511-9512; `camOffset.set(0.62…, 0.92…, 0.62…).normalize()` at steep 0, 9524, which puts the camera on the south-EAST diagonal at a 46.4° pitch; 32° vertical FOV, 9571) at the JSON's stamped match-seconds, WITHOUT the follow lerp `camFollow.lerp(tmpV, 1 - Math.exp(-5.0 * dt))` (9566), which lags the camera ~0.2 s behind the arithmetic — so the frames show the bathhouse a little more centred at u75 than the numbers say. They are labelled as arithmetic wherever used; the pictures are what I judged.

## VERDICT: NO-SHIP
The canvas frame at u100 is a genuine establishing shot — a six-tier lit bathhouse dead centre in a red market under an indigo sky, unmistakably this world and the one frame in the pack I would put in the App Store — but what the child sees over it is the full HUD from frame one, no title card at all on this instrument (the card runs on the wall clock, the shot on the match clock), a crowd bubble at 2.06 s with the controls damped, and a 1.3-second stretch in the middle of the 3.6-second dive with neither the bathhouse nor the void in frame, over a canal painted with large pale discs; all of it closes with the shared fixes the Maple and Game Day chairs already filed, plus one Lantern-only decision below.

## THE BAR
**Mario Kart Tour** (Nintendo, iOS) — the shipped mobile opening with exactly this shape: every race opens on a flyover that HOLDS on the course's landmark, dollies along the track under the course name set small and off the subject, with NO race HUD, and cuts to the kart on a still camera; the HUD arrives with the 3-2-1, when the hands matter. Every frame of that flyover has a subject. **Donut County** (Annapurna, iOS 2018) for the hole-game arrival: pulled back on the whole scene, a beat of hold, an ease down onto the hole, the level captioned small and off-centre, never over the hole. For a NIGHT opening specifically, **Alto's Odyssey** (Snowman, iOS): the title sits in the sky band above the horizon, never on the ground the player will ride — the sky is where a night level's name belongs. **hole.io** (Voodoo) has no establishing shot at all, so this world beats the genre by having one.

Where LANTERN NIGHT sits: the SUBJECT and the FIRST FRAME are at the bar (a landmark worth opening on, centred, readable at camDist 246, and the palette says the world's name before the card does). The MOVE is under it: a hold, a fast slide off the landmark, then 1.3 s of a subject-less canal, then the void for the last 1.1 s — a flyover with a hole in its middle, and the "corridor" the code promises (1431-1433) is never on screen as a corridor. The CARD is under it by code (centred on the landmark, on the wrong clock; unseen in every page frame). The FRAME is under it (a live HUD over a cinematic, the clock already running, a bubble before the hands matter). One thing Lantern gets right that Maple and Game Day did not: nothing is eaten before the child touches the screen — the coin chip reads "✦ 0" and the growth track is empty in every frame through settled.

## IS THIS THE BEST THIS CAN BE?
No — but the first frame is, of the four I could compare against the sibling packs' descriptions, the most "this world" of any: `lantern_u100_canvas.png` answers the brief's test (what is it OF, could it be any world, would you put it in the store) with a lit tower, a red market, a crowd with lanterns, bamboo and a night sky. Ranked, what stands between this and the bar:

1. **One clock** — card, HUD fade and bubble mute on `body.intro` and the Web Animation (fixes 1, 3, 4). Until then the child sees a HUD-laden cinematic with a name card that may or may not be there, and no probe can gate the card's placement.
2. **The card off the tower** (fix 2): the name on the bamboo horizon, the lit bathhouse under it. Then frame one is the Alto's Odyssey composition — title in the sky band, the world below — with Mario Kart Tour's HUD timing.
3. **The middle** (fixes 5 and 6): a decision first — accept a subject-less canal and make it read as water under lanterns, or hand over early and buy the child 1.8 s on a still void. Either is one shoot away from being judged; neither is a constant to tune blind.
4. **The sky** (fix 7): a red moon over the bathhouse is the frame's missing top-right; LIGHT's call.

After 1-2 the page frame at u100 is the canvas frame plus a name over the hill and no HUD, and u0 is the void on a still camera with the HUD fading up — that is the bar. Lantern also arrives at u0 with nothing eaten and nothing to apologise for, which is more than two of its siblings can say.
