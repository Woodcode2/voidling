# TEAM HERO — the void in the first frame (Stream D, round 5)

## VERDICT: NO-SHIP
In every world the title card prints the world's name across the hero's face during the one moment that is his — the arrival — and on Maple, the first world a child ever sees, the shot has no subject at all: it opens on a 20-pixel speck and holds there.

## THE BAR
**Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, course intro.** Mechanically: a ten-second fly-over of the course's landmarks with the course name card up over the LANDMARK footage; the card is gone before the cut; the cut lands on a clean frame behind the racer, idle at the line, nothing drawn over the character; the first interaction cue (the start lights) sits at the top of the frame, not on the hero. Zero frames of hero-under-text. The hero is the payoff of the sequence, and the frame he is revealed in is the cleanest frame of the whole intro.

## IS THIS THE BEST THIS CAN BE?
No. Ranked, what is between here and the bar:
1. **F1** — the card off his face. One CSS block; it is the whole difference between "the hero is revealed" and "the hero is the wallpaper behind the level name". Everything else is second to this.
2. **F2** — Maple gets a subject. The first world a child sees is the only one without an establishing shot; the other four already prove the mechanism works.
3. **F3** — Pirate's spawn off the purple floor. The one world where his identity colour does not hold at the moment he is handed to the player.
4. **F4** — an arrival beat. Slosh and blink as the camera lands, so the settle is his and not just the camera's.
5. **F5** — nothing eaten, nothing paid, before the first touch. His first act should be the child's.
After 1-4 the reveal matches the bar mechanically: name over the landmark, cut to a clean frame, hero idle-alive at centre, the interaction cue (∞) under him and off the card. What is left after that is a question for LIGHT/GROUND (the scrim's darkening of the whole centre while the card is up — moving it to the top band removes it from the hero, the top band then needs to look intended) and for CHOREOGRAPHY (whether the 4.2 s card and a 2.2 s Maple intro should share one length).
