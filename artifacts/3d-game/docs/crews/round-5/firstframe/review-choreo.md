## VERDICT: NO-SHIP
The card, the camera, the controls and the crowd all arrive in the same second: on Maple and Pirate Bay the controls go live at 2.2 s while the title card is at full opacity with a 0.72 scrim over the exact centre where the void and the ghost hand are, the first crowd bubble fires at ~2.0 s under the card on all five worlds, and the match clock has already spent 2–4 s of the child's 180 before she can touch anything.

## THE BAR
**Mario Kart 8 Deluxe (Nintendo, 2017), course intro.** Mechanically: a fly-over of the course; the course-name card is shown over the fly-over and is GONE before the camera settles behind the kart; then a three-beat countdown with audio ticks on a still camera; controls go live on GO. Card, camera settle, and control-live are three sequential events; no two share a frame, and any button skips the fly-over straight to the countdown. **hole.io (Voodoo, 2018), the genre bar:** no establishing shot at all — gameplay camera from frame 0, controls live on frame 0, exactly one teach channel (the hand performing the drag) and the timer already running because frame 0 is playable.

**Where we sit** (the code says, `src/prototype3d.ts:5583-5585` and `index.html:548,558`): the card is a fixed 4.2 s CSS animation on the WALL clock — opacity 1 from 0.59 s (14%) to 3.02 s (72%), zero at 4.2 s — while the shot runs `COPY.introLen` MATCH seconds (2.2 / 2.2 / 3.4 / 3.6 / 3.5). On a phone (match-s ≈ wall-s) the card's opacity at the frame the controls go live is **1.00 Maple, 1.00 Pirate, 0.68 Game Day, 0.60 Powder, 0.51 Lantern**, and it outlives the shot by 2.0 / 2.0 / 0.8 / 0.7 / 0.6 s. The three events MK8D keeps apart land together here, plus a fourth (crowd speech) that MK8D never has and hole.io never has under a card. Nothing skips the shot: a touch during it is a damped drive (69% of full input, `qa/_introdrag.mjs`, round-3 skeptic), not a skip.

A note on reading the pictures: the probe's card runs on wall time and the software renderer's match clock runs slow, so in every `*_u0.png` the card is already faded (Maple u0 at tPage 2.59: the "MAPLE FALLS" ghost is at roughly a quarter opacity over the void). **On the phone it is at full opacity at that frame.** The frames under-state finding 1; they do not create it.

## IS THIS THE BEST THIS CAN BE?
No. Ranked, what stands between here and the bar:
1. **Sequence the three events** (finding 1): card → camera settle → lesson, in match time, never sharing a frame. This is the whole difference between our opening and MK8D's, and it is ~15 lines.
2. **Hold the crowd until the child is on the ground** (finding 2): one line in `calm()` and one number.
3. **Answer the first touch** (finding 3): 3× intro clock on pointerdown. It makes every other wait in this report optional, which is what a hypercasual child expects.
4. **Shoot the first-ever run** (finding 5): the instrument photographs the wrong player today; until `--first` exists nobody has seen the frame §2D asks about.
5. Clock (4), words (6), label (7).
After 1–3 the opening is: a 0.6 s card-in over the landmark, a hold, a dive that ends as the card leaves, a still camera, and one hand — which is hole.io's opening with a card in front of it, and MK8D's order. What would still be short of the bar is the countdown beat MK8D uses to load the first input: the hand is silent. AUDIO's surface, but choreography would ask for one tick as the hand lands.
