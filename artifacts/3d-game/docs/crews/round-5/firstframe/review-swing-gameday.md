# GAME DAY — the establishing shot, u100 → settled

Cinematographer, Stream D (brief §2D). Pack: `docs/crews/round-5/shots/firstframe/gameday_*.png`, numbers from `docs/crews/round-5/firstframe-data/gameday.json` (SEED=7, 430x932@2, introLen 3.4, hero = the stadium at 3D (-3.5, -140), spawn at 3D (-2.5, 140.5) — the code says `STADIUM = { cx: 5930, cy: 3200 }` (gameday.ts:84) and `GD_SPAWN = [5950, 8810]` (gameday.ts:296), so the subject is 280.5 units due north of the void).

Every camDist and hand-over fraction below is computed from the code's own constants (`camDist = 38 + 262·k²`, prototype3d.ts:9493; `q = (u-0.25)/0.5, e = q²(3-2q)`, 9511-9512; 32° FOV and 46-62° pitch from the WorldCopy doc at 1247-1248) at the JSON's stamped match-seconds. ## VERDICT: NO-SHIP
The shot is the best first frame in the game — a real stadium at centre, the tailgate lot around it, a treeline and sky, the world's crimson everywhere — but what a child sees over it is a live HUD from frame one, a name card that runs on a different clock from the dive (and was absent from every page frame in this pack), crowd speech before the controls exist, and a void that eats a cup before anyone has touched the screen.

## THE BAR
**Mario Kart Tour** (Nintendo, iOS) — the shipped mobile opening that does exactly this shape. Every race opens on a course flyover: the camera holds on the course's landmark, then dollies along the track, under the course name set small and off the subject, with NO race HUD; the cut to the kart lands on a still camera, and the HUD (positions, items, lap) arrives with the 3-2-1, when the player's hands matter. Every frame of the flyover has a subject; nothing on screen during it is interactive. **Donut County** (Annapurna, iOS 2018) for the hole-game arrival: pulled back on the whole scene, a beat of hold, then an ease down onto the hole, the level captioned small and off-centre, never over the hole. **hole.io** (Voodoo) has no opening shot at all — it starts on the gameplay camera — so this world beats the genre by existing.

Where GAME DAY sits: the SUBJECT and the FIRST FRAME are at the bar (a landmark worth opening on, in frame, readable, unmistakably this world). The MOVE is one notch under (a 0.8 s stretch mid-swing with neither landmark nor hero in frame — a flyover with no subject). The CARD is under it (centred on the subject, and on the wall clock while the shot is on the match clock). The FRAME is under it (a live HUD over a cinematic, the clock already running, crowd bubbles before the hands matter). One eat fires before the child exists.

## FINDINGS

### The card and the shot are on two clocks — and the card was in none of these frames
SEVERITY: major
AT: index.html:548 (`#titlecard.show { animation: cardFade 4.2s ease forwards; }`), index.html:557 (`@keyframes cardFade { 0%{opacity:0…} 14%{opacity:1…} 72%{opacity:1} 100%{opacity:0…} }`), src/prototype3d.ts:5582-5585 (`tcEl.classList.add('show'); titleUntil = tClock + 4.6; … introT = COPY.introLen;`), src/prototype3d.ts:8527 (`const dt = Math.min(0.05, dtRaw);`), src/prototype3d.ts:9460 (`introT -= dt;`), src/prototype3d.ts:1408-1411 (`// 3.4 still finishes inside the 4.2s title card`)
SAW: no "LEVEL 3 / GAME DAY / the whole town turned out · eat the tailgate" in any of `gameday_u100.png`, `gameday_u75.png`, `gameday_u50.png`, `gameday_u25.png`, `gameday_u0.png`, `gameday_settled.png` — the swing sheet `gameday_swing.png` shows six frames of HUD and picture with no card anywhere. The Maple reviewer saw the card at u50, u25 and u0 on the same instrument.
EVIDENCE: the code says the card is a CSS animation on the WALL clock (`cardFade 4.2s`) and the dive is on the MATCH clock, which advances at most 0.05 s per rendered frame (`Math.min(0.05, dtRaw)`). They coincide only above 20 fps. Under the software renderer the u100 page frame is already 0.35 match-s in — at least 7 frames after the card was armed — and at the rates this probe runs (the governor's rule 4: "about 14x slower than wall time") 0.35 match-s is ~5 s of wall time: the card had faded to 0 before the first sample. Maple's intro is cheaper and ran fast enough for the card to survive; Game Day's did not. The code's own count says why: "the opening frame renders 4,694 draw calls and 1.40M triangles against 1,241 and 355k in settled play" (prototype3d.ts:9462-9464). On a phone, the same coupling means: if the intro's frames run under 20 fps, the dive stretches and the world's name leaves before the shot lands. The comment "3.4 still finishes inside the 4.2s title card" is true at ≥20 fps and false below it — and nobody has measured Game Day's intro frame rate on the owner's iPhone.
FIX: put the card on the match clock. Replace the CSS animation with a paused Web Animation driven from the intro tick. prototype3d.ts:5582: `tcAnim?.cancel(); tcAnim = tcEl.animate([{opacity:0, transform:'translateY(10px) scale(0.98)', offset:0}, {opacity:1, transform:'none', offset:0.14}, {opacity:1, offset:0.72}, {opacity:0, transform:'translateY(-6px) scale(1.02)', offset:1}], {duration: 4200, fill:'forwards', easing:'ease'}); tcAnim.pause(); tcT = 0;` and in the frame tick beside `tClock += dt` (8533): `if (tcAnim) { tcT += dt; tcAnim.currentTime = Math.min(4200, tcT * 1000); }`. Delete `#titlecard.show { animation … }` (index.html:548) and keep the keyframes for reference or drop them. Same 4.2 s on a 60 fps phone; on a 12 fps intro the card now waits for the dive, and `titleUntil = tClock + 4.6` (already match-clock) finally agrees with what is on screen. Experiment alongside, on the owner's phone: log `dtRaw` (already fed to `perfFrame`, 8528) for the first 3.4 s of a Game Day match and report min fps — that number decides whether this is latent or live for him today.
GATE: qa/firstframe.mjs, per moment, record `getComputedStyle(document.getElementById('titlecard')).opacity` and `performance.now()` into the JSON; assert at u75 and u50 opacity ≥ 0.9, at settled (introLen + 1.0) ≤ 0.05. ## IS THIS THE BEST THIS CAN BE?
No — but the picture is. `gameday_u100_canvas.png` is the strongest first frame in the five worlds by the brief's own test (what is the frame OF; could it be any world; is there a frame you would put in the App Store): a stadium, a tailgate, a crowd with a direction, one colour, a treeline, a sky. Ranked, what stands between this and the bar:

1. **One clock.** Card, HUD fade and bubble mute all keyed on the match clock (`body.intro` + the Web Animation). Until then the card is a wall-clock guess laid over a match-clock shot, and no probe can gate its placement. Fixes 1, 3, 4.
2. **The card off the subject** (fix 2) — the name above the bowl, not on it. Then the composition is Mario Kart Tour's: landmark, name over the sky-and-trees band, HUD arriving with the hands.
3. **Nothing eaten before the hands** (fix 5). One cup, but it is the first thing that happens in the match and the child did not do it.
4. **A shorter subject-less middle** (fix 6) — an experiment, one constant, judged by eye.
5. The sky (fix 7), LIGHT's call.

After 1-3 the page frame at u100 is the canvas frame plus a name in the treeline band, and at u0 it is the void on a still camera with the HUD fading up — that is the bar.
