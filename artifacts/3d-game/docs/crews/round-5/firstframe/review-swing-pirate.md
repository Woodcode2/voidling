# PIRATE BAY — THE ESTABLISHING SHOT, u100 → settled

Cinematographer's review, Stream D (FABLE-LAUNCH-BRIEF §2D). SEED=7, 430×932@2, `qa/firstframe.mjs`.
Every "I saw" below is from a named PNG; every "the code says" quotes the line. Nothing was built or run.

## VERDICT: NO-SHIP
The hold quarter of Pirate Bay's opening is aimed at the Royal Mariner's authored site and the Royal Mariner is not there — the establishing shot is of sand and a food truck — and the literal first frame a phone shows (a 36-unit-high horizon shot from the cove, then a 250-unit backwards launch to orbit) has never been photographed, because the instrument's first sample lands at 0.39 match-s.

## THE BAR

**Mario Kart Tour (Nintendo, 2019), the course intro.** Mechanically: a 4–5 s authored flyover of the course's landmark at constant altitude, the course name card in a corner of the frame (not over the subject), the race HUD hidden for the whole flyover, then a CUT — not a dolly — to the kart on the grid, and only then the countdown and the HUD. Two shots, one cut, one card, no HUD.

**Angry Birds (Rovio), the level open.** The grammar we are actually attempting — "here is the target, and here are you" — done as a lateral pan at ONE scale from the pigs' fort to the slingshot in ~1.5 s, so the child learns where the target is relative to them. The two subjects share the frame during the pan.

**hole.io (Voodoo), the genre's default.** No intro at all: the match opens at gameplay distance with the timer and leaderboard live. Which is why our HUD-on-from-frame-one is not automatically wrong — but hole.io does not put a flyover under it.

**Where Pirate Bay sits.** We try to do Mario Kart's flyover AND Angry Birds' hand-over in one continuous 8× dive (camDist 300→38, `prototype3d.ts:9493`) while panning 352 units in 1.1 s (hero (127,−115) → spawn (47.5, 228); `island.ts:1380`, `island.ts:235`). The frame is 0.265·camDist wide on the ground, so at camDist 300 it is ~80 units wide: at NO moment of the swing can the hotel and the void share the frame. The hand-over the shot exists for cannot be read. Round 3 said this from code (`STUDIO-ROUND-3.md:835`, "320 u/s whip pan … there is no frame where the hotel is settled"); the pictures now say something worse — the hotel is not in the frame it is held on.

## IS THIS THE BEST THIS CAN BE?
No. Ranked, what stands between here and Mario Kart Tour's opening:
1. **Put the hotel back in the shot** (1). Without the subject there is no establishing shot to review; every other note is about a frame of nothing.
2. **Seed the camera on its orbit** (2). Then photograph frame one for the first time and judge it — it may want its own hold before the dive begins.
3. **One shot, one card, one hand-over**: the card off the hero and ending with the intro (3), 3.4 s so the pan can be read (4b), the HUD dark until the controls are live (5). After these three the swing is a flyover with a name on it, which is the bar.
4. **The frame worth an App Store slot** does not exist yet. The closest in the pack is `pirate_u75_canvas.png` — turquoise bay, three boats, the pier, a red cabana — and it has neither the hotel nor the void. The hold frame after 1 and 2 (cream hotel with teal roofs, the galleon in the bay to its left, the coast to its right, "PIRATE BAY RESORT" on the sand above) is the one to shoot for the store, and it costs nothing beyond fixes 1–3.
5. The spawn's distance to the coast (4a) is a level-design decision; the reshoot after 1–3 should decide it with the settled frame in hand.

What is already right and must not be touched in fixing the above: the void's purple against the magenta-and-blue dance floor in `pirate_u0_canvas.png` reads at a glance — the character is findable the instant it arrives; the ferris-wheel shadow across the floor gives the settled frame a second layer for free; the palette (sand, turquoise, coral roofs, the navy of space) says "resort" in every frame that is not space.
