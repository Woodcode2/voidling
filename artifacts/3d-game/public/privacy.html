The Cute World Ender — Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 August 2026

The Cute World Ender is made for children. It does not ask for a name, an email address, an age, a photo, or a sign-in. It has no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no advertising. We do not build a profile of any player and we do not track anyone across other apps or websites.

What stays on the device

Everything you unlock is stored on your own device, in the browser's local storage or the app's local sandbox. That includes:

  • coins, level, XP and high scores
  • which skins you own and which one is equipped
  • your daily-login streak and which quests you finished
  • sound, rumble and stats preferences

None of it is sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing site data in the browser, erases all of it permanently — there is no cloud copy and we cannot restore it.

Anonymous statistics — off unless a grown-up turns them on

The Cute World Ender can send anonymous gameplay statistics that help us find bugs and work out which parts of the game are too hard. This is switched OFF by default. It can only be switched on from Settings, and that row sits behind a parental gate (a multiplication question a young child cannot answer). It can be switched off again at any time, in the same place.

When — and only when — it is switched on, we send:

  • the name of the moment (for example match_end, skin_view) and its time
  • gameplay values such as world, score, match length and frame rate
  • the app version and whether it is running on iOS or the web
  • one coarse hardware speed bucket — low, mid or high
  • a random session number that is created when the game starts and never written to storage, so it cannot be linked to any earlier or later session

There is no persistent identifier of any kind. Earlier versions of this app created one; it has been removed, and any copy left on a device is deleted when the game loads. We do not collect the advertising identifier, precise screen dimensions, device model, IP-based location, or contacts. These statistics are stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, purely so we can query them; they are never sold, never shared for advertising, and never used to identify a person.

Purchases

Optional skins can be bought through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles the payment; we never see or store card details, billing addresses or Apple ID information. The purchase button and the RESTORE PURCHASES button both sit behind the same parental gate. Purchases can be managed or refunded through your Apple account settings, and Apple's Ask to Buy / Screen Time controls apply as normal.

Hosting and artwork

The game's artwork is delivered from a content delivery network. Like any website, those servers receive the ordinary technical information a browser sends in order to deliver a file — an IP address and a user-agent string. We do not use that information to identify anyone and we do not combine it with anything else.

Children's privacy

The Cute World Ender is made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age. Because there is no account and no persistent identifier, there is nothing tied to a child to disclose, correct or delete — clearing the app's data removes everything. If you believe we have somehow received personal information about a child, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also appear in the app's release notes.
